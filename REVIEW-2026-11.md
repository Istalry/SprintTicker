# SprintTicker — design review after the 2026-11 redesign

**Date:** 2026-11-13 (redesign phase P15a).
**Material:** the 31 screens of `node scripts/doc-images/capture-app.js
--screens <dir>`, captured on a throwaway profile under `--mock-hardware`
with the invented data of `pnpm docs:images`; the target design
(`Documentation/design-history/Redesign-2026-11-screens.html`, its words
table above all); the renderer's source.
**Method:** three independent reviews, each by its own agent and skill:
`design-critique` (C), `ux-writing` against the glossary (W) and
`accessibility-audit` against WCAG 2.2 AA (A). Every finding was checked
against a screen and the code; anything that could not be confirmed was
dropped. Nothing was run under a screen reader.

Like `AUDIT.md`, this is a dated snapshot: a finding's text stays as it was
written, and only its **status** moves. Where two reviews found the same
thing, both ids are given and the finding is listed once.

Statuses: **fixed** (with the sub-phase), **to fix** (the sub-phase that
will), **decision** (the user's, asked), **listed** (seen and kept, with the
reason).

## 4. Questions on the design itself

Raised by the critique; not defects.

- **Interrupt with nothing running** (S4): "⚡ Interrupt" with no task to
  interrupt reads oddly to a newcomer. — listed: S4 draws it so, and the
  first-run sentence explains it; worth a first-time user's try.
- **The Place window** is 600×640 for one row. — to fix (P15a6): sized to
  its rows, with a maximum.

## 5. What already works

From the accessibility review, kept so the next change does not undo it:
landmarks and `aria-current` on the places; no `div` click handlers; the
running timer is not a live region; the switches (`SettingRow`) with an
On/Off word and a visible focus ring; no state shown by colour alone; the
text tokens pass on their normal surfaces; no target below 24 px without
the spacing exception.
