Take a closer look at the tech specs of your BUSY Bar and explore its hardware capabilities.

# Design and materials

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/3StCFqarJkJQZV-7N79yY/YEDeyKRz5sFD3l2i8swrl_design-and-materials.png?format=webp "Document image")

-   **Color:** White body with orange accents, black front and back panels

-   **Body material:** PC/ABS

-   **Main screen material:** PC

-   **Back panel material:** PC

-   **Light guide material:** PC

-   **Padding material on the monitor mount:** TPE

# Size and weight

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/3StCFqarJkJQZV-7N79yY/T3QagZJaAl8ieWGjXw14v_busy-bar-sizes.png?format=webp "Document image")

-   **Depth:** 40.8 mm (1.61 inches)

-   **Weight:** 250 grams (8.82 ounces)

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/3StCFqarJkJQZV-7N79yY/mpeAgVg5HkdgsWy407qJZ-20260422-114944.jpg?format=webp "Document image")

-   **Physical size:** 158.4x35.2 mm

-   **LED size:** 1.23x1.2 mm

# Back display

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/3StCFqarJkJQZV-7N79yY/xgp3wRORJwgxnW4cFNVRg-20260422-115443.jpg?format=webp "Document image")

-   **Type:** Monochrome OLED (white)

-   **Diagonal:** 1.54 inches

-   **Max refresh rate:** 60 Hz

-   **Colors:** 16 grayscales

-   **Resolution:** 160x80 pixels

-   **Controller:** SSD1320

-   **Interface:** SPI

-   **Brightness control:** Manual or automatic (via light sensor)

# Main MCU

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/3StCFqarJkJQZV-7N79yY/6IVT8hDNAvX1h9_TA_Qkc_busy-bar-mcu.png?format=webp "Document image")

-   **Model:** [STM32U595RI](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32u595ri.html)﻿

-   **Core:** Arm Cortex-M33 @ 160 MHz + MPU, FPU, and DSP

-   **Flash:** 2 MB

-   **SRAM:** 2.5 MB

# Wireless MCU

-   **Model:** [Silicon Labs SiWG917M111MGTBA](https://www.silabs.com/wireless/wi-fi/siwx917-wireless-socs/device.siwg917m111mgt)﻿

-   **Core:** Arm Cortex-M4F @ 180 MHz + MPU, FPU, and NWP

-   **Flash:** 8 MB

-   **SRAM:** 672 KB

# Wireless connectivity

-   **Wi-Fi version:** Wi-Fi 6 (IEEE 802.11 b/g/n/ax)

-   **Wi-Fi band:** 2.4 GHz only

-   **BLE version:** 5.4

# Data storage

-   **Type:** eMMC NAND flash memory

-   **Capacity:** 8 GB

# Speaker

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/3StCFqarJkJQZV-7N79yY/HULa9bGN5rUZF6mxojX2u-20260415-110809.jpg?format=webp "Document image")

-   **Audio mode:** Mono

-   **DAC model:** NS4168

-   **DAC input interface:** I2S

-   **DAC sample rate:** 44.1 kHz

-   **Amplifier type:** Embedded in DAC

-   **Amplifier class:** D

-   **Speaker impedance:** 6 ohms

-   **Speaker power:** 0.8 W

# Battery

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/3StCFqarJkJQZV-7N79yY/I6-gqamleN33_43Ikx51W-20250912-150044.jpg?format=webp "Document image")

-   **Type:** 1-cell 18650

-   **Capacity:** 3250 mAh

-   **Battery connector:** A2007-H04 (4-pin)

-   **Protections:** Overcharge, over-discharge, overtemperature, undertemperature

-   **Charging time:**

-   2.5 hours — with an 18 W (or higher) Power Delivery charger

-   7–8 hours — with other USB chargers or a USB port

# USB-C port

-   **Function:** Virtual LAN adapter and charging port

-   **Mode:** USB device with Power Delivery (PD) support

-   **Version:** 2.0 Full speed

-   **Max speed:** 12 Mbit/s

-   **PD version:** 3.0

-   **PD supported modes:** 5V⎓3A, 9V⎓1.5A
