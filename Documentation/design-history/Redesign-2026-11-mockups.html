It opens on its own the first time. One question per screen, a test before going on, and a way through without any account. It ends on Today with the picker open.

4 · Picking a task, and ending one

The picker opens on a search and lists tasks grouped by project, instead of two steps. From the tray, the mini timer or the deck it opens on its own, without bringing the whole app forward. Ending uses three words everywhere: Not done, Done, Discard.

5 · Outside the window: mini timer, prompt, tray, toast

A prompt never takes the focus. The stand-up waits on the mini timer and on Today until it is answered. Without either, a toast opens SprintTicker on it. The tray speaks plainly and opens on a single click.
